GMP convention (Theorem 8.2)
For every N >= 23, this family has exact source complexity N and exact complement complexity 2N-1 in GMP's acceptance-anywhere model. GMP Lemma 3.1 provides a no-added-state normalization. Its final state supplies a fixed backward channel; removing that channel preserves faithfulness and strengthens the transferred lower bounds. Adding one fresh globally final state to each of the main-model constructions attains both bounds. Thus this language cannot establish exact GMP 2N. An additive constant preserves the leading factor, but it cannot be omitted from an exact claim.
